# Changelog

## 0.3.0

- Interface in nine languages: English, Russian, Chinese (Simplified),
  Spanish, Portuguese, Japanese, German, French, Korean. Dates follow the
  language.
- "Runs out" names the day when it is not today.
- Sessions tab: each row drawn as heavy as its share of the spend, the
  subagents' part and a missing cache flagged, a line on top with the top
  sessions' share and the peak hour.
- `USAGE_METER_DIR` moves the data folder; the pane shows it with `~` for the
  home folder.
- Icon, homepage, keywords and screenshots.

## 0.2.0

- Sessions tab: tokens per hour, totals and the heaviest sessions.
- `tools/backfill.py` recovers session spend from past transcripts.
- English and Russian interface, picked from Claude Code's language setting.

## 0.1.0

- Limits recorded from the engine's own reports, charted with a forecast.
